# Theorem OS research-decision closure — 2026-11-03

## Result

Each current research publication shown to Lead now needs its own explicit plan decision. The accepted decision records the publication ID, task and finding digests, outcome, reason, affected task IDs or source paths, time, and accepted plan version in the run archive. An unchanged plan or objective-only rewrite cannot claim adoption. A source-edit decision remains pending until the changed owned Lean file reaches the canonical commit. Neither decision is proof acceptance.

The Lead sees at most eight current publications per turn. Their exact IDs are fixed at dispatch; later arrivals wait for the next turn, and a ninth existing publication is carried forward instead of being hidden by the input-digest deduplication. A saved plan replayed without its original action must still answer pending findings before it can be applied. Ordinary workers do not receive the Lead's decision records in their context projection.

Module identities M4 and M5 were advanced for future attempts. Existing frozen run packages, campaign checkpoints, credentials, and protected histories were not modified. No paid model or mathematical-answer database was used for validation, and no campaign was restarted.
